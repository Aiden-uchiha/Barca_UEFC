The Official Club History

Five Eras

Barça UEFC's legacy spans five distinct competitive eras — marked by dynasty-building, heartbreaking collapses, miracle rebuilds, and unmatched global dominance.

I
Era I

Foundations & The Rubypool Surge

Where it all began — a club still called Rubypool, built on one star and a wave of elite talent.

The Brazilian Pro League & TheRin

The club's earliest competitive foundation was built in the Brazilian Pro League, anchored by TheRin. When family obligations forced TheRin to step back and retire from regular play, the club faced its first major transition.

The Nexus & Second Brazilian Cup Dynasty

Stepping onto a massive stage in Nexus, Barça attracted a wave of elite talent — Legit, Bunny, Real Z, Isagi demon king, Aiku, Error, Water, lorenzo.kx, Carla and Void. Managing a stacked roster of world-class stars required careful lineup rotation, which paid off immediately as Barça secured a 2nd-place finish in the Nexus League — their final achievement under the Rubypool banner before rebranding to Barça UEFC.

The Brazilian Challenge

Taking a 7-man squad into the fiercely competitive Brazilian Cup (Legit, Real Z, Aiku, Dragonovedtv, Mikey | 303, TheRin and lorenzo.kx), Barça accepted the local challenge and dominated. Legit played like an unstoppable monster despite high ping, teaming up with Real Z on offense and Aiku in goal to secure the title.

🏆 Brazilian Pro League 🥈 Nexus League 🏆 Brazilian Cup
II
Era II

The Unstoppable Duo & The Velterra Heartbreak

The highest peak the club had ever reached — and the collapse that nearly ended it.

Peak Dominance

Entering the Velterra Cup, Barça's win rate skyrocketed. The team was spearheaded by the unstoppable duo of Bunny — a pure, lethal shooter — and Legit, an all-around force who defended, passed, assisted and scored at will.

The Final Collapse

Reaching the Velterra Cup Finals against one of the era's biggest teams, disaster struck in the first leg. With manager Dragonovedtv delayed, internal friction flared over starting spots, resulting in a 5–2 loss. Despite returning with Legit to win the European second leg, internal player scandals and a grueling Asian-server third leg led to an overall series loss and a 2nd-place finish.

💔
The Exodus: Following the heartbreak, egos and demands for playtime and individual awards fractured the locker room, causing the star-studded roster to leave for rival clubs.
🥈 Velterra Cup The Unstoppable Duo: Bunny & Legit
III
Era III

The Miracle Rebuilds

Left for dead twice — rebuilt twice. The era that proved the badge mattered more than the names.

The Neo Champions Circuit

Left with almost no players, the club entered the Neo Champions Circuit with remaining loyalists Rusue, Cold Prince and RndmX. Entering a prime stretch of form, Rusue single-handedly engineered comebacks and carried the depleted roster to an improbable 2nd-place finish. Rusue was rewarded with the MVP of the Season and Best Player of Barça.

The New Gen Prime (Elite League)

After Rusue departed, Barça recruited a fresh wave of Brazilian and international "New Gen" prodigies — fenomas, Genius, arian and Cold Prince — rejoined by veteran Aiku. Genius proved clinical in front of goal, sweeping the Ballon d'Or and Golden Boot, while fenomas dominated every leg with tackles, assists and goals to earn the Playmaker award.

🧤
The 50-Save Miracle: Goalkeeper arian cemented his place in Barça history during an El Clásico clash against Re AL, recording over 50 saves in a single leg — one of the greatest defensive performances ever seen — propelling the New Gen squad to a 1st-place league title.
🥈 Neo Champions Circuit 🏆 Elite League The 50-Save Miracle
IV
Era IV

The Golden Legacy

Shikai, Yuzarf, the UECL run and the World Cup — the era that made Barça immortal.

Forging the Greatest Partnership

Returning to Nexus, the club recruited Shikai, the Turkish powerhouse Yuzarf, goalkeeper Sarox and striker Gencay. After a rocky debut against the league's strongest squad, the team clicked — highlighted by a 10–0 El Clásico demolition of Re AL and a 3rd-place league finish.

Yuzarf — The GOAT

Widely recognized as the greatest player in club history for his sheer individual takeover ability and clutch gene.

Shikai — The Best Player & Maestro

The most effective player in Barça history, serving as the tactical heart of the squad and unlocking unmatched team chemistry alongside Yuzarf.

UECL Silver

Directly following the Nexus campaign, Barça made a deep run in the UECL (Unmatched Ego Champions League), finishing as runners-up in 2nd place. The four-player squad was Dragonovedtv, Sarox, Shikai and Yuzarf. Shikai won both the Golden Boot and Barça's Best Player award, while Yuzarf claimed the Playmaker Award.

World Cup Conquest

Taking the Barça core onto the international stage with Japan (Barça), the squad dismantled the group stage with routine 10–0 victories. In a clash against the Nexus League champions — without their starting keeper — emergency signing Tuna delivered a masterclass in goal to upset the tournament favorites. Everyone was shocked, because they thought it was impossible to beat them — until Japan (Barça) humiliated them 10–4.

⚡
The Semifinal Miracle vs France: Trailing 5–1 after the first leg with Yuzarf absent, Barça faced near-certain elimination. Arriving late for the second leg, Yuzarf scored 5 goals in 3 minutes to stun France in a legendary comeback — before Barça swept all four legs of the Finals to claim the World Cup.
🥉 Nexus 🥈 UECL 🌍 World Cup
V
Era V

The Treble & Total Domestic Supremacy

Perfection, a treble, and then a reload that never missed a beat.

USL Season 1 Perfection

Entering the inaugural Uzbekistan Super League (USL) without Gencay, Barça achieved total perfection — going undefeated, setting the all-time USL record for highest Goal Difference, averaging 10 goals a game, and never conceding more than four, with Hyper anchoring the net.

UEL & Cup Lower-Bracket Runs

In both Unmatched Egoist League (UEL) Season 1 and Unmatched Egoist Cup Season 1, Barça suffered rare early knockout setbacks when key players missed matches — only to storm through the lower brackets, defeat the teams that knocked them out, and claim both trophies to complete a historic Treble.

USL Season 2 & The Anakin Era

After a hard-fought 2nd-place finish in a grueling USL Season 2 (hampered by device issues and Yuzarf's absence in the final), Yuzarf departed for a rival club. Barça reloaded by signing European star Anakin alongside Hyper (both playing in Asia alongside Asian star Taski). Operating as a relentless goal machine, Anakin joined forces with Shikai to sweep UEL Season 2, the Uzbekistan Super Cup and Unmatched Egoist Cup Season 2 without dropping a beat.
